Our Mission

Our mission is to make traditional Oriental medicine and holistic healthcare knowledge accessible, practical, and relevant to modern daily living. We share well-researched educational insights that promote disease prevention, balanced nutrition, mental harmony, and sustainable well-being.

We believe that understanding the close connection between daily habits, lifestyle routines, mind-body balance, and spatial harmony (applied feng shui) empowers individuals and families to make informed, responsible health choices.

Traditional Medicine & Health Education

Exploring foundational traditional medicine concepts, herbal properties, and preventive wellness traditions for public education.

Healthy Lifestyle & Preventive Awareness

Sharing guidance on balanced nutrition, physical activity, circadian rest, and non-pharmacological stress management.

Metabolic & Reproductive Health

Providing educational resources regarding metabolic balance, digestive harmony, and reproductive wellness.

Holistic Living & Spatial Feng Shui

Practical cultural perspectives on living spaces, environmental balance, and peaceful living conditions.

Application Overview & Google Services Integration

Google OAuth App

The Kim Long Đường Content Publisher application is an internal workflow tool developed and maintained by Next Tutor as the authorized technical operator to assist Lương y Phạm Xuân Kim in managing and publishing educational video content directly to our official YouTube channel. The Google Cloud project and API credentials belong to Lương y Phạm Xuân Kim, with technical operations managed under authorization by Next Tutor.

YouTube Data API Integration Details

When authorized, our application connects to the YouTube Data API v3 strictly under the following requested scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — Used solely to upload scheduled video files, set video titles, descriptions, and educational tags to the authorized Kim Long Đường YouTube channel.

Data Usage Guarantee: Our application does not read private comments, does not access personal emails or Google Drive files outside designated workflow assets, does not sell user data, and never uses Google API data to train generalized AI/ML models. Access credentials and tokens are kept strictly internal, never shared externally, and stored securely in our private automation environment.
